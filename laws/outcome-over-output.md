---
name: Outcome over output
severity: warning
---
**Name the outcome, not only the artifact.** A task states what changes for a user or the business when it ships. If the outcome cannot be stated, the work is not ready to start.

Bad: "Add an export-to-CSV button."
Good: "Triagers audit offline, cutting triage time from 4 to 2 minutes, through a CSV export."
